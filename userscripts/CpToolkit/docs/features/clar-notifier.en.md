# Clarification notifications

[Back to the feature list](../../README.en.md#4-feature-list) ・ [日本語](clar-notifier.md)

> Notifies you when the organizers post an answer or a correction during a contest.

## How it works

- Notifies you on screen when the organizers post an answer or a correction (clarification) during a contest
- While you are not looking at AtCoder, a desktop notification is also shown
- Checks once a minute during a contest, but not in the first 5 minutes

## Detailed settings

Change these under "Detailed settings" of each feature in the settings.

| Setting                                                   | Default |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| Use desktop notifications while you are away from AtCoder | On      |
